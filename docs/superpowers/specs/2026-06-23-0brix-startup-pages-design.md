# 0brix Startup Pages Design

## Goal

Refresh the public 0brix homepage and collaborators page so the product feels like a useful construction-tech startup with a working product behind it. The pages should make the product value clear to contractors while inviting investors, advocates, operators, and partners to help scale the project.

## Homepage

The homepage positions 0brix as "infraestructura comercial para contratistas." The first viewport explains the product in practical terms: 0brix converts construction signals into qualified prospects that users can review, save into a private CRM, and follow up on. The page includes:

- Primary CTA to `/swipe`.
- Secondary CTA to `/homeowner-intake`.
- Collaboration CTA to `/colaboradores`.
- Product visual showing a lead card and CRM stages.
- Sections for Swipe, CRM por usuario, Homeowner intake, and the Detecta -> Califica -> Guarda en CRM -> Da seguimiento workflow.

## Collaborators Page

The collaborators page presents 0brix as an investable and distributable startup project. It separates contribution paths clearly:

- Capital for market expansion and B2B acquisition.
- Divulgacion for creators, media, and audiences around construction, real estate, startups, and small business.
- Operadores locales who understand local permit and contractor markets.
- Data partners who can improve freshness, validation, contactability, and close probability.
- Sales partners who already sell to contractors.
- Product collaborators for growth, AI, and workflow improvements.

## Visual Direction

The pages use a restrained SaaS style: light surfaces, dark product hero, red/blue/green accents, compact cards, stable responsive grids, and clear product CTAs. The design avoids purely decorative visual noise and uses the product mockup itself as the main visual asset.

## Verification

Static tests assert the required positioning, product CTAs, workflow language, collaboration paths, and contact email. Deployment verification checks production HTML from `https://0brix.com/` and `https://0brix.com/colaboradores`.
